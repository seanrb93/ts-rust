Brick throw: the brick is thrown instead of dropped.
In the game the brick leaves the hand at 0.2 a frame; with the game's gravity (0.0083 a frame, a frame) it is on the floor within a metre, so it can hardly hit anyone.  This mod gives it
the speed of the launcher's grenade (0.85) and a lift (throw_lift 0.07, added to its upward speed), a smaller spread, and the same damage.
Measured on the game's own numbers (gravity, the 0.99 a frame drag): the brick is level with the crosshair within 0.3 units up to 14 units away, 0.6 below it at 18, and lands at about 25.
The throw_lift field is the port's own (weapons.txt of a mod): the lift is added to the upward speed of the shot.
