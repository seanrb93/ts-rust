First person hands: the first person guns are held by the arms of the player's own character, as they would be held in real life.  The right hand is on the grip with its fingers bent round it, the left hand is palm up under the front of a two handed gun (or on its foregrip) with the arm coming in from the lower left, and mines, TNT and the brick lie on the upturned palm.  The arms are the character's own, with its own skin and sleeve.
The mod holds no guns.  Its folder arms has the arms of each of the 66 characters with a model (aliens, robots and mutants included) and how each gun is held; the program puts them on a gun when the gun is loaded (ts-game/src/manual/armmerge.rs), so the mod is under 2 MB, and the gun is whichever the packs have: with the Smooth Weapons mod on, the smooth one.  A character added by a mod has the first character's arms.
Run `python examples/first_person_hands_src/make-hands.py <game>/PAK/OB.PAK <game>/mods/first_person_hands_mod` to make the folder (about ten seconds; it comes from the game's own models, so it is not kept in the repository).  WEAPONS in that file lists the guns and where the hands go.
Limits: split screen shows the first player's arms in every view, and the hands do not move for a reload.
Dual wielding: when a gun is held in each hand, the guns have only the hand on the grip, so a two handed gun does not show four arms, and the gun of the left hand has a left hand.  Going from a gun to its pair or back, the gun in the right hand dips out of view while its model changes.
Which model the game loads is the script of the mod, scripts/hands.rhai (docs/scripting.md): gunN_cNN.raw for the first player's character, with _l or _m for a gun held in each hand.  Those are the names the program makes; there are no such files.
