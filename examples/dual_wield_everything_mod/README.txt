Dual Wield Everything: every weapon has a (x2) version in the weapon sets, and an unarmed player has a left fist as well as a right.
The whole mod is a script, scripts/dual_wield.rhai (docs/scripting.md), that says which pairs the weapon page lists and the sets may have, and that the fists take turns.  Switch it on from the Mods page (or enabled = true) and start the game again.
