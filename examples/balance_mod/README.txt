Balance: a rebalance of the game's weapons by data only (damage, the size of pickups, the most ammunition carried).
The damage was set from the measured rates of fire (docs/damage.md): a full hit on the body does about 1 to 1.2 damage per second for the sidearms, 1.4 to 1.7 for the automatics and rifles, 2.5 to 3 for the shotguns at point blank; the Colt and the snipers keep their few strong hits.
The stores: pistol ammunition 500 -> 200, rifle 200 -> 160, shotgun 100 -> 50, plasma 200 -> 150 (about 15 to 60 seconds of continuous fire, the game gave 20 to 160).  The kinds are shared by their weapons.
It only changes the weapon table.
The bots' pause between shots (weapon table field +60, which only the bots read) is raised to about the rate the player is held to by the fire animation: Pistol 2 -> 19 frames, Shotgun 12 -> 25, Colt 20 -> 38, Mauser 10 -> 11, Scifi Handgun 6 -> 8, Assault Shotgun 15 -> 19, Raygun 10 -> 15, Assault Rifle 6 -> 7, and the slow weapons to about 59.  Measured with bots holding only a pistol or a shotgun: the gaps were 2 to 3 frames (pistol) and 12 (shotgun) in the game, and are 18 and 24 at the least with this mod.
